# Cedar Bridge Case Overview

## Case Reference
CB-001

## Case Type
Fictional cybersecurity investigation.

## Background

Cedar Bridge is a fictional organization used for cybersecurity learning and portfolio development.

The scenario involves unusual authentication activity associated with a fictional employee named Amara. Three failed login attempts were followed by a successful login from an unfamiliar source. A subsequent attempt to download a restricted payroll file was denied.

## Investigation Objectives

- Review the supplied authentication records.
- Identify unusual login activity.
- Understand the difference between authentication and authorization.
- Assess potential risks to sensitive business information.
- Identify evidence gaps.
- Recommend appropriate investigative steps.

## Initial Assessment

The authentication sequence is suspicious and requires further investigation. However, the available records do not prove that the account was compromised or that payroll information was disclosed.

## Evidence Limitations

The assessment uses supplied fictional records. No live investigation or real-world incident response was conducted.

## Disclaimer

All organizations, people, devices, assets, and events in this case are fictional and intended for educational purposes.
