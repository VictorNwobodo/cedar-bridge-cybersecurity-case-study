# Cedar Bridge Asset Risk Assessment

## Purpose

This register identifies the fictional organization's important assets, their security requirements, potential threats, weaknesses, impacts, and recommended controls.

## Asset 1: CUSTOMER01

- Asset: Customer contact list
- Owner: Customer Service
- Primary security requirement: Confidentiality
- Threat: Unauthorized access or disclosure
- Potential weakness: Excessive user permissions
- Potential impact: Exposure of customer information and loss of trust
- Recommended controls: Role-based access control, least privilege, and access logging

## Asset 2: BILL01

- Asset: Invoice register
- Owner: Finance
- Primary security requirement: Integrity
- Threat: Unauthorized modification
- Potential weakness: Weak editing restrictions or inadequate change monitoring
- Potential impact: Billing errors, financial losses, and customer disputes
- Recommended controls: Restricted editing permissions and audit trails

## Asset 3: PAY01

- Asset: Payroll export
- Owner: Finance Manager
- Primary security requirement: Confidentiality
- Threat: Unauthorized access or download
- Potential weakness: Optional or unenforced multifactor authentication and potentially excessive permissions
- Potential impact: Disclosure of employee salary information
- Recommended controls: Enforced multifactor authentication, least privilege, and download logging

## Asset 4: PORTAL01

- Asset: Customer ordering website
- Owner: Operations
- Primary security requirement: Availability
- Threat: Service disruption
- Potential weakness: Insufficient monitoring or resilience
- Potential impact: Lost orders, reduced revenue, and customer dissatisfaction
- Recommended controls: Availability monitoring and tested recovery procedures

## Assessment Note

These are preliminary risk observations for a fictional scenario. The listed weaknesses are potential concerns and should not be interpreted as confirmed vulnerabilities in a real organization.
